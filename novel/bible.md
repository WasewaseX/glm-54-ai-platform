# THE DARK GENOME — Story Bible (Book One: Greyharbor Bloom)

> ORIGINAL WORK. Inspired only by GENRE CONVENTIONS of progression fantasy / occult mystery / biopunk
> (shadow realms, secret societies, tiered power systems, sanity costs). It must NOT reproduce or imitate
> any specific copyrighted characters, names, systems, sequences, potion lists, spells, organizations,
> or plot text from "Shadow Slave", "Lord of the Mysteries", or any other work. Everything here is invented
> for this book. Writers must not import terminology from any existing novel.

## LOGLINE
In the fog-locked port city of Greyharbor, a mortuary assistant is infected by a parasitic shadow-organ
during a bio-mystical cataclysm's anniversary — and must climb a ladder of forbidden biological tinctures
to stop the dark biosphere bleeding into the world from devouring his city.

## SETTING
- **Greyharbor** — a rain-and-fog industrial port city, canals, abattoirs, slate rooftops. Gaslight meets
  early microbiology era (brass microscopes, chalkboards, quarantine bells).
- **The Sporefall** — seven years before Book One: a luminous spore cloud blanketed Greyharbor for one
  night. Half the sixth district vanished. Since then, the fog never fully lifts and curfew holds at dusk.
- **The Hollow** — a dark mirror-biosphere overlaid on the city, reached through **Folds** (spatial scars).
  Vast fungal canopies, bioluminescent rot-gardens, streets grown over with mossed bone. Not empty: it is
  inhabited and organized.
- **The Court of Lanterns** — the Hollow's elegant, inhuman nobility. They ride grown carriages of chitin,
  speak in perfume and debt, and consider human beings "seed-stock". Key figures: **Lady Wren of the
  Molted Court** (poised, honest in the way of knives), **the Lantern King** (never clearly seen; his court
  orbits his silence).
- **The Veridian Concord** — a hidden fraternity of apothecary-biologists who study the Hollow and fight its
  tide. Structure: local **Apothecary houses** (Greyharbor's is House Blackwing), governed by the **Circle
  of Leaves**; field agents called **tallymen**; codex-walkers are their elite.
- **The Slate Market** — Greyharbor's occult black market under the fish exchange: memory vials, contraband
  essence, false tinctures. Fence-in-chief: **Marrow**, a gray-cheerful dealer who never lies and never
  tells the whole truth.

## POWER SYSTEM — THE LACUNA AND THE TINCTURES (fully original)
- **The Lacuna** — a parasitic extra organ that some Sporefall-exposed humans grow. It sits behind the
  sternum, drinks light, and hungers for **essence** — the shadow-stuff of Hollow creatures. A host who
  feeds the Lacuna is a **gravid**.
- **Essence Tiers** — progression ladder from **Tenth Essence** (newly gravid) up to **First Essence**
  (near-mythic). Each tier grants one new **Organ** (a supernatural biological power) and one new **hunger**.
  Tiers advance by consuming essence AND surviving the organ's growth fever. Glands unlocked grow new
  senses: taste of fear, sight through smoke, voice that stills hush-hounds.
- **Tinctures** — brewed extracts (Hollow biology + reagents + a memorized **gene-script**) that stabilize
  an organ and let a gravid choose their **Codex** (specialization). Brewing requires a licensed
  apothecary's bench; rogue brews cause **splicing** (fusion of host and monster — the **Amalgam Heresy**).
- **Codices** — Predator (hunt, speed, kill-sense), Chirurgeon (flesh-shaping, healing, unmaking),
  Botanist (spore-craft, growth, domain over Hollow flora), Symbiont (pacts with Hollow organisms).
  A gravid swears one Codex at Ninth Essence; the Codex writes itself into their genome.
- **The Fade** — the price. Every tier burns memories into the Hollow: scents first, then faces, then
  names. The Hollow is built — literally — from everything the Fade takes. Memory is the only currency
  that both worlds accept.
- **The Warden** — the vast colony-mind at the bottom of the Hollow: a chorus of every consumed memory,
  wearing the faces of the forgotten. The Hollow is its body; the canopy its ambition.
- **The Seeds** — the deepest secret: seven designed gravid lineages, made before the Sporefall by a
  Concord botanist. Soren is the Seventh Seed — grown, not chosen.

## MAIN CHARACTERS
- **Soren Vale** (POV) — 23, mortuary assistant, dry gallows humor, careful hands, orphaned at sixteen when
  his mother **Maerwen Vale** vanished during a quarantine sweep. Wears her stopped pocket-watch. Arc:
  survivor → tool → skeptic → the one who rewrites the system. Hunts like a predator but keeps count of
  everything — a habit from tallying bodies.
- **Ilse Vann** — Concord tallyman, 40s, iron-gray braid, counts everything twice, lost her brother to the
  first tide. Becomes Soren's hard, fair mentor. Maimed (left hand) in ch. 8; sacrifices herself at the
  Rootgate (ch. 24). Her brass tally-counter is Soren's memory-anchor.
- **Dax Merrow** — fellow recruit, 19, quick grin, chronically broke, brews unsanctioned tinctures to pay
  his mother's rent. Betrays the Concord under Court pressure (ch. 19) and dies redeeming it, leaving
  Soren his mother's wedding ring — a memory-token that later anchors Soren against the Fade.
- **Lady Wren** — Court noble, patronly and predatory, tells the truth in ways that wound. Knew Maerwen;
  offers Soren apprenticeship twice. Her motive: the Hollow is starving and she knows it.
- **Primate Aldous Crane** — Concord Primate of Greyharbor, Purist, kind eyes, granite convictions,
  secretly negotiating a "controlled ascension" with the Court (ch. 25 betrayal).
- **Maerwen Vale** — the mother, present only in memories, letters, and the truth of the Seeds: she meant
  the Lacuna to heal the Hollow (re-seed it), not to feed it.
- **Marrow** — Slate Market fence, gray, cheerful, deal-maker; recurring comic-dark relief with an
  encyclopedic knowledge of who owes whom.

## CREATURE BESTIARY (Hollow fauna — invent freely in this style)
lantern-mites (swarming light-eaters), hush-hounds (pack hunters that steal sound), the Mummers (faceless
thieves of expression), gallows-walkers (praying-mantis carrion giants), the Choir (a migrating colony that
sings in stolen voices), glasswing moths (dream-drinking), a rootgate leviathan (the Rootgate's keeper).

## VOICE & STYLE GUIDE (mandatory)
- Third-person limited, past tense, locked to Soren. Cinematic, sensory, grounded; horror through
  specificity (the smell of a wound, the count of a heartbeat), not through adjectives.
- Dialogue-forward: characters argue, bargain, joke under fear. No purple sludge; every paragraph earns
  its place.
- Webnovel pacing: every chapter ends on a hook (reveal, turn, threat, decision). Cliffhangers are welcome.
- Chapter length: **1,400–1,700 words**. Chapter title format: `## Chapter N — Title` then prose paragraphs
  separated by blank lines. Scene breaks: a line containing only `* * *`.
- Banned: real-world brands, modern slang beyond era-neutral voice, any phrase or name lifted from any
  existing published novel, emoji, markdown lists inside prose. Use em dashes sparingly.
- Continuity: track Soren's current Essence tier, which Organs he has, what the Fade has taken, dates
  (Book One spans autumn: the anniversary of the Sporefall is the night of ch. 1–2).
