# THE DARK GENOME — Book One Chapter Outline (30 chapters + epilogue)

ACT I — SPOREFALL (1–8)
1. The Body in the Fog — Mortuary night shift; curfew bells; Soren preps a drowned sailor and finds a
   blooming, light-drinking organ nested in the corpse's chest. His stopped pocket-watch ticks once.
   Hook: the organ opens an eye.
2. Lacuna — The organ grafts onto Soren in a dream-hunt; he wakes with a heartbeat behind his sternum and
   the taste of smoke he never smoked. The city's fog leans toward him. Hook: a hush-hound is inside his
   locked room.
3. Greyharbor Below — First Fold-walk into the Hollow overlay of his own street: fungal canopy, mossed
   bone, lantern-mites. He kills a hush-hound with a shovel and, starving, eats its spilled shadow —
   Tenth Essence taken; new hunger wakes. Hook: something tall applauds from a balcony of galls.
4. The Tallyman — Ilse Vann finds him by the essence-stink; recruitment or vivisection; she explains
   gravid, Lacuna, tiers, the Concord. Soren chooses the door with the leash he can see. Hook: she shows
   him the missing-persons wall — his mother's face is on it.
5. Tincture of the Novice — House Blackwing; first brew under supervision; Codex choice at the bench.
   He swears Predator. Growth fever; first Organ (the kill-sense: he can feel which heartbeat in a room
   will die first). Hook: Ilse warns him the Fade always takes the kindest memory first.
6. Curfew Hunters — A tide breaches during the anniversary curfew; Soren's first field harvest with the
   cell; Dax Merrow introduced (saves Soren's life, immediately bills him for it). A courtier of lanterns
   watches from a rooftop and leaves a perfumed calling-card. Hook: the card is addressed to "the Seventh".
7. The Missing Sixth Floor — The mortuary's sixth floor is a Fold; inside, a garden of teeth and a seer's
   message grown into the wallpaper, addressed to Soren by name: "THE SPOREFALL WAS PLANTED. ASK THE
   CONCORD WHO SOWED." Hook: his reflection stays behind one heartbeat too long.
8. First Essence Burned — Court raid on the safehouse; Lady Wren's thralls; Ilse loses her left hand;
   Soren's Predator instinct blooms mid-fight (Ninth Essence) and he kills a thrall with his kill-sense
   and a mortician's table lift. Act break: the Concord owes him answers; he owes the Court a visit.

ACT II — THE CONCORD (9–18)
9. Apothecary Blackwing — The Concord deeps; Circle of Leaves hierarchy; genome-craft school (gene-
   scripts, scribing desks, failed students in sealed wards). Soren learns how tinctures actually work.
   Hook: a sealed corridor labeled with his mother's botanical seal.
10. The Ledger of Flesh — Field catalog in the Mycelium Fields of a vanished harvest-team; finds
    fusion-carcasses — human and Hollow genomes spliced; the Amalgam Heresy is real and new. Hook: the
    spliced are still alive and counting the team out loud, in Ilse's brother's voice.
11. Dax's Bargain — Dax is brewing unsanctioned tinctures to cover his mother's rent; Soren covers for
    him; the two bond; the Fade takes Soren's memory of his mother's face — he keeps the watch but not
    her. Hook: Dax's buyer pays in Court perfume.
12. The Botanist's Letter — Deciphering the seer's message with a Concord archivist: the Sporefall was
    engineered — the math matches Concord gene-script, forty years old. Hook: the script's signature
    glyph is Maerwen's mark.
13. Whispers in the Slate Market — Espionage in the black market; Marrow sells him a vial of First
    Essence at a terrible price (a memory of the sea); Soren burns it to Eighth Essence; new Organ
    (smoke-sight). Hook: Marrow's ledger shows Concord Primate Crane buying Court perfume for a year.
14. The Chirurgeon of Ashlane — A rogue Chirurgeon grafts Hollow organs into desperate civilians; Soren
    mercy-kills a fused child; captures the Chirurgeon; the man was paid by the Court to "practice".
    Hook: among his papers, a list of seven names — the seventh is Soren's.
15. Court of Lanterns — Infiltration of a Court masque in the Grand Canopy; chitin carriages, perfume
    debts, the Lantern King's silent orbit. Soren steals a genome-ledger. Lady Wren unmasks him in
    private — and offers apprenticeship: she knew Maerwen. Hook: "Your mother is not dead, little seed.
    She is inventory."
16. The Mother Hypothesis — Soren confronts the Circle; the Concord's sealed corridor holds his mother's
    work: the Seeds — seven designed gravid lineages — and the plan's two readings: heal the Hollow or
    feed it. The Council orders the corridor re-sealed. Act break: Soren stops trusting anyone who
    counts him as an asset. He is Eighth Essence; the Court and the Concord both want the Seventh.
17. Schism — The Purist–Amalgamist split goes open inside House Blackwing after the ledger leaks; wards
    burn; Soren refuses both banners, keeps Ilse and Dax; the Circle splits Greyharbor's defense. Hook:
    a district vanishes overnight — the Hollow is feeding on the city directly now.
18. The Hungry Canopy — The Hollow's expansion accelerates; refugees; Concord and militia armed truce;
    Soren organizes harvest-cells like a mortician running triage; grows to Seventh Essence (Organ: the
    Fold-step). Hook: the canopy above the docks has begun to whisper his name in his mother's voice.

ACT III — THE CANOPY WAR (19–30)
19. Siege of Blackwing — Court assault on House Blackwing; Dax, cornered by a year of debts, opens a
    sally-gate — then burns the Court's boarding-line and dies redeeming it, leaving Soren his mother's
    wedding ring. Hook: the Court was not trying to win. It was buying time for something at the Rootgate.
20. Lady Wren's Garden — Wren, bound by truce after the siege, shows Soren the truth in her garden of
    galls: the Hollow is starving; the Sporefall was a lifeboat for it; the Court's plan is to drag
    humanity into the canopy as seed-stock. Hook: she confirms a Concord Primate is negotiating a
    "controlled ascension" — who, she will not say.
21. The Seventh Seed Blooms — Soren's Lacuna matures: he can open Folds without a door; he glimpses the
    Warden — a horizon of faces, all forgotten, all singing. Hook: the Warden stops mid-song when it
    notices him, and every face turns.
22. The Warden's Voice — First parley with the Warden through a Fold-dream: it offers succession — become
    the canopy's mind, keep Greyharbor as a garden. It demonstrates that it is made of the Fade: it wears
    Maerwen's face because it ate her memories. Hook: it shows him the night of the Sporefall — from the
    other side.
23. Gathering of Codices — Concord war-council under armed truce; the plan: burn the **Rootgate** — the
    Hollow's root-anchor under the harbor — to starve the canopy before the Court's "ascension" tide.
    Soren is elected point because only a Seed can walk the deep root-roads. Hook: Crane blesses the
    expedition with tears in his eyes — and sends a sealed letter to the Court.
24. The Rootgate March — The descent through the deep Hollow; bestiary war (Choir, gallows-walkers,
    glasswing swarms); Ilse holds the rearguard at the Choir's crossing and does not come back; her
    tally-counter goes to Soren, already ticking. Hook: the Rootgate is not a gate. It is a nest.
25. Betrayal at the Gate — Crane's letter revealed: the Court's ascension tide arrives with Concord
    banners; Crane wants a "managed" feeding — the city's poor first. Both sides turn on Soren when the
    Court's herald names the Seventh Seed before the armies. Hook: Soren opens a Fold and steps out of
    the battle, alone, toward the nest.
26. Court of One — Soren vs. Court vanguard and Crane's tallymen in the root-roads; he accepts the
    Predator genome to its end (near-Fade); anchored by Ilse's counter and Dax's ring, he kills the
    herald and shatters the ascension tide's anchor-line. Hook: the Warden opens the nest for him —
    gently.
27. The Warden's Succession — Inside the Warden's core: the god's biology revealed (a colony of every
    consumed memory); the succession offered openly. Soren finds Maerwen's final memory sealed inside:
    the Seeds were meant to re-seed the Hollow with human memory — symbiosis, not feeding. The Court fed
    it; the Concord locked it; both starved it. Hook: the Warden, for the first time in an age, asks
    instead of takes.
28. Greyharbor Rises — Above: militia and truce-armies hold the dockline as the canopy stills. Below:
    Soren refuses the throne and does the harder thing — he brews, with the Warden's consent, the first
    true New Codex tincture: a hybrid genome of Warden and human memory, and re-seeds the canopy. The
    Hollow's hunger turns inward; the expansion dies. Hook: the Warden gives him back a scent — the sea,
    the one he sold.
29. The New Codex — Aftermath and reckoning: Crane arrested by his own Circle; Wren chooses exile above
    with her court rather than starve; the Concord reformed around the New Codex under Soren's veto; the
    Fade partially reverses for the re-seeded — his mother's face returns to his memory at last, from the
    inside of the Hollow's gift. Hook: of the seven Seeds, six are accounted for.
30. First Light in Greyharbor — The fog lifts for the first time in seven years; the city sees the sun;
    Soren keeps the mortuary, keeps the watch ticking, keeps the vigil. Quiet denouement with Ilse's
    counter on the sill and the city breathing. Final hook: a Concord letter from an overseas house —
    a seed-signal answered there too.
EPILOGUE. The Overseas Letter — 700–900 words. A distant port's apothecary house receives the signal and
opens a sealed case marked with Maerwen's glyph. Book Two hook. No cliffhanger violence; leave wonder.
