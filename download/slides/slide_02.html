This slide is the quarter in one view: exceptional growth across all of our key metrics. Starting with revenue — twelve point three million dollars in ARR, up eighty-five percent year-over-year. In practical terms, revenue has nearly doubled compared to last year. Customer growth was just as strong: one hundred and twenty new enterprise customers joined the platform this quarter, and retention remains one of our structural advantages. On product, we launched three new AI modules, bringing the platform to twelve in total — that expanding roadmap is directly strengthening our competitive position and is a big reason win rates keep improving. And our market position now stands at number two in enterprise AI platforms with eighteen percent market share, while remaining the fastest-growing company in the segment. The line at the bottom says it all: we are accelerating AI adoption for enterprise customers.
