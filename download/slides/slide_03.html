Now the financial detail behind those headlines. Quarterly performance is accelerating: Q3 revenue was three point one million dollars, up twenty-two percent quarter-over-quarter — and as the trajectory chart shows, every quarter in the last two years has built on the last. ARR now stands at twelve point three million, up eighty-five percent year-over-year. Two things matter about the quality of this growth. First, gross margin is seventy-eight percent and stable — we are delivering healthy gross margins while still investing aggressively in sales and R&D, so growth is not being bought with unprofitable revenue. Second, discipline on burn: we have eighteen months of runway at the current burn rate, and the milestone card on the right is the one I would highlight — we are on track to reach profitability by Q1 2025, and on the current trajectory, ahead of schedule.
