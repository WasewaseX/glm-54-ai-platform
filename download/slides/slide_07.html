We expanded sales and marketing aggressively this quarter to capture new opportunities — but importantly, we did it while improving efficiency, not at its expense. On the sales side, we grew the enterprise team by forty percent, entered two new verticals in Healthcare and Financial Services, and cut the average sales cycle by twenty-five percent, so more sellers are closing deals faster. On the marketing side, brand awareness is up sixty percent on share of voice, and marketing efficiency stands at a one-dollar-twenty CAC-to-LTV ratio, which validates that spend is disciplined. Channel partnerships are becoming an increasingly important part of our growth engine: fifteen new strategic partnerships signed this period, and channels now contribute thirty percent of revenue. The funnel you see on the left is healthy end to end — more leads, sharper qualification, and faster conversion into customers.
