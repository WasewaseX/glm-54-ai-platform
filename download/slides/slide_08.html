Our roadmap balances near-term execution with a clear long-term vision. Over the next two quarters we will launch industry-specific AI solutions, expand into European markets, and complete a full overhaul of our API platform — deepening the core while opening new segments. Through the remainder of 2024 we take the next step: launching the AI marketplace, integrating our strategic acquisition target, and expanding internationally into APAC. The acquisition is deliberately sequenced to accelerate capabilities we would otherwise need years to build in-house. Long term, the ambition is singular: to become the definitive enterprise AI platform, with one hundred million dollars in ARR by 2026. Every initiative on this page ladders up to that goal — industry-specific solutions, global reach, and a continuously enhanced core platform.
