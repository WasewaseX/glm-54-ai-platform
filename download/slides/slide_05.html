Finally, the product engine behind these numbers — these innovations directly impact our value proposition. We launched three new modules this quarter: the Advanced NLP Processing Engine, Multi-Model AI Orchestration, and a Real-time Analytics Dashboard. Each addresses key customer needs we heard consistently across enterprise deals — deeper language understanding, the flexibility to route work across models, and live operational insight. Just as important are the platform-level improvements: our proprietary inference engine has cut compute costs by thirty percent, and API latency improved by forty percent. These performance gains enhance unit economics directly — every dollar of revenue now costs less to serve. We also expanded our security framework to FedRAMP compliance, which opens doors in regulated industries. And customers are noticing: a ninety-four percent satisfaction score and an average four point seven out of five rating on G2.
