Nearly half of revenue now comes from existing customers expanding their usage
The customer metrics show we are expanding the enterprise base while retaining and growing it at the same time. Total customers reached three hundred and forty-two, up sixty-eight percent year-over-year, with one hundred and twenty new logos in the quarter alone. Enterprise customers grew even faster — one hundred and eighty-seven, up ninety-two percent — so enterprises are now the majority of our base. Average contract value climbed to sixty-five thousand dollars, up fifteen percent, as customers deploy more of the platform across their organizations. Retention is the foundation underneath: net revenue retention of one hundred and forty-five percent demonstrates the increasing value customers derive as their usage expands — on average, existing customers spend forty-five percent more with us each year. Churn fell to five point two percent, down from eight point one percent a year ago, and forty-five percent of total revenue now comes from expansion.
